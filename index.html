Customer SLA—▸
SLA boundary = the binding constraint at each temperature: RH-max curve vs. dew-point cap (whichever is lower) on top, RH-min vs. dew-point floor on the bottom. Leave dew-point cap blank for an RH-only box. All boundaries recompute at site pressure. ASHRAE TC 9.9 reference ramp limits: 9°F/hr (5°C/hr) where tape media is present; 36°F/hr (20°C/hr) otherwise.

Inverse psychrometrics at site pressure —. A slung or aspirated instrument reads the psychrometric wet bulb (WMO CIMO formula, needs ≥3 m/s over a wet wick); ASHRAE Eq. 35 gives the thermodynamic wet bulb, a chart property. Reading one as the other biases RH low by ~0.5 points — systematic, so it will not average out. Wet bulb can never exceed dry bulb. Pass bands: ±2% RH typical capacitive-sensor spec, ±5% marginal — recalibrate beyond that.
