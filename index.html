About

Built by linkedin.com/in/tahoggatt for data center critical-environment teams. Source on GitHub.

  • Psychrometrics: ASHRAE Handbook — Fundamentals (2021), Chapter 1: saturation pressure (Hyland–Wexler), humidity ratio, enthalpy and specific volume. Dew point and wet bulb are solved from their definitions, not shortcuts.
  • Your site's real pressure: from elevation (ASHRAE Eq. 3) or a measured barometer. Every property and envelope is computed at it.
  • Checked, not assumed: against CoolProp's ASHRAE RP-1485 real-gas model at 3,898 points, worst humidity-ratio error 0.0013 %, with IAPWS-95 as a second source. The planner re-runs a self-test every time it opens.
  • Envelopes: ASHRAE TC 9.9 (Recommended, A1–A4), plus your own SLA limits and ramp rates.
  • Timing a move: a moisture mass balance on the hall's air against your plant's rates, scaled by efficiency and today's derates. The slowest limit, plant or SLA ramp, sets the time.
  • CDU calculator: a counterflow ε–NTU plate exchanger, with glycol and water properties fitted to CoolProp.
